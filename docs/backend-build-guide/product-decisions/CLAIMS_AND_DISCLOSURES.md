## Statements permitted by implemented contracts

- Catalogue/market read APIs may describe reference identity and an explicitly labelled Estimated Market Value only where Documents 006–007 provide it with source, `asOf`, confidence, and data status.
- Public collector and Vault Live responses are limited to approved safe projections; they do not establish ownership, custody evidence, or provider relationships.
- Portfolio is explicitly unavailable until Document 013; it must not show fabricated holdings, cash, P&L, allocation, or performance.
- A Document 010 submission may be described as private draft, submitted, under review, changes requested, approved, rejected, or cancelled according to its durable workflow state.

## Statements prohibited or deferred

- Do not call a Document 010 approval “verified”, “authentic”, “valued”, “secured”, “insured”, “published”, “on-chain”, or “tradeable”.
- Do not imply custody, insurance, valuation, ownership, fractional availability, returns, distributions, or live order activity from a submission.
- Do not name a storage, custody, insurance, valuation, or payment provider unless a later approved integration supplies evidence and an allowlisted public contract.
- Do not expose object keys, storage paths, filenames, checksums, scan reports, private reviewer notes, policy/provider references, credentials, or internal audit metadata.
- Do not present local test scanning or local object storage as production scanning or a production storage provider.
