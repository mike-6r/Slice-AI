# Entity and database blueprint

Existing identity/control models are scaffolded in Prisma; all others are proposed and owned by one prompt. All IDs are opaque UUIDs, timestamps UTC, private fields excluded from public DTOs, and financial/ownership history is append-only.

Key cross-model invariants: category/set/grade compatibility (006); only published eligible assets reach issuance (011→012); ownership supply and reservations conserve integer units (012); every financial transaction balances integer minor units and lots reconcile to ownership (013); execution settles both ledgers atomically (014); distribution lines sum exactly to net proceeds (015); provider events post money once and reconcile before production enablement (016); business mutation and outbox commit together (017).

Document 015 completion: `ExternalSaleVerificationApproval` is unique per verifier/sale and forces two distinct non-proposer approvals; append-only vote history retains one effective vote per immutable eligibility snapshot; distribution lines and reconciliation runs are durable and do not repair authority.
